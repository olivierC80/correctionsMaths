💡 Synthèse utilisable à l’oral

“This report clearly connects technology, environment, and inequality.
As Mandela once said, fighting poverty is an act of justice — and energy access is part of that fight.
Africa’s solar revolution could reduce inequalities, but as Joseph Stiglitz would remind us, globalization still means dependency: 80 % of solar panels come from China.
The challenge now is to make this technology both clean and fair.”
