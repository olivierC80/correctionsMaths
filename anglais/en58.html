💬 7. Phrases prêtes à placer à l’oral

  • Technology gave us power — now it asks if we deserve it.
  • The question is no longer “Can machines think?” but “Can we still feel?”
  • The future is not about machines replacing us — it’s about machines redefining us.
  • Artificial intelligence is a mirror — it reflects our own fears and desires.
  • Transhumanism turns evolution into a choice, not a destiny.
  • In creating machines in our image, we may be losing what makes us human.
