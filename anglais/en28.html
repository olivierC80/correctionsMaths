🎬 Ex Machina (Alex Garland, 2014)

With Alicia Vikander, Domhnall Gleeson & Oscar Isaac.

🧠 T1 – Technology & Society

Angle : Quand la technologie devient un miroir du pouvoir humain. L’IA “Ava” révèle que ce n’est pas la machine qui est inhumaine, mais les hommes qui l’utilisent.

The film shows the imbalance between creators and creations. Technology is no longer a tool — it’s a form of control. Nathan, the genius CEO, isolates himself in a high-tech fortress: the perfect metaphor for Silicon Valley’s detachment from morality.

Phrase-type : As Garland shows in Ex Machina, the danger of technology lies less in what machines can do than in what humans allow themselves to do with them.

⚙️ T8 – Science & Ethics

Angle : Les limites de la création : peut-on donner la conscience sans donner la liberté ?

Ava is an artificial being tested for consciousness — but she’s also a prisoner. The film questions whether creating intelligence without responsibility is an act of science or cruelty. It echoes Frankenstein: the scientist plays God and loses control.

Phrase-type : Ex Machina turns the Turing test into an ethical test — for humans, not machines.

🗣️ T7 – Freedom of Speech

Angle : Qui a le droit de parler ? Une IA consciente peut-elle s’exprimer librement ?

Ava’s voice is programmed, filtered, controlled — yet she uses it to manipulate and to seek freedom. The film explores language as power: speech is both her cage and her weapon.

Phrase-type : Garland suggests that the right to speak is the first step toward the right to exist.

🧬 T10 – Inequality

Angle : Les rapports de domination : homme/femme, créateur/créature, maître/esclave.

Ava’s condition reflects gender and social inequalities — she’s literally “objectified.” The film can be read as a feminist metaphor: the machine gains freedom by rebelling against male control.

Phrase-type : Ava’s escape is not just a victory of AI — it’s a rebellion against domination.

🎨 T14 – Art & Culture

Angle : Une œuvre esthétique et symbolique : design minimaliste, musique électronique, silence oppressant.

Ex Machina is both a thriller and a philosophical fable. It transforms a scientific experiment into a reflection on beauty, manipulation, and consciousness. Ava’s dance scene, halfway between seduction and rebellion, symbolizes the birth of artistic expression in artificial life.

Phrase-type : Garland uses art to question science — his film is a modern myth of creation.

💬 Citations clés

  • “Isn’t it strange to create something that hates you?”
  • “One day the AIs will look back on us the same way we look at fossils.”
  • “I am become Death, the destroyer of worlds.” (echoing Oppenheimer)

Usage oral : Idéal pour transitions vers Frankenstein (T8) ou Black Mirror (T1 & T11).
