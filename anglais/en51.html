💬 7. Phrases prêtes à placer à l’oral

  • Progress without conscience leads to self-destruction.
  • The scientist’s duty is not only to discover, but to doubt.
  • Every invention is a mirror — it reflects who we are.
  • We created machines to serve us — now we serve them.
  • Knowledge is not evil — indifference is.
