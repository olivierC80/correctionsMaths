📘 Brave New World (Aldous Huxley, 1932)

🧠 T1 – Technology & Society

Angle : La technologie au service du contrôle social.

Huxley imagines a world where people are created in laboratories and conditioned to be happy. Science replaces religion, and pleasure replaces thought. Technology doesn’t liberate — it pacifies. Society is efficient, but at the price of freedom and individuality.

Phrase-type : As Huxley shows in Brave New World, the most effective form of control is not oppression — it’s satisfaction.

🌍 T2 – Environment

Angle : La nature bannie au profit de la perfection artificielle.

Nature is considered “dirty” and “inefficient.” People prefer the comfort of machines to the unpredictability of life. It’s a warning against over-controlling the world and losing our connection to it.

Phrase-type : In Huxley’s world, nature is replaced by order — and order kills wonder.

🗣️ T7 – Freedom of Speech / Thought

Angle : Liberté et conditionnement : les gens croient être libres, mais ils ont été programmés pour aimer leur servitude.

There is no censorship because no one wants to think differently. People are “happy slaves.” The “feelies” (films that stimulate all the senses) anticipate today’s mass entertainment.

Phrase-type : Freedom disappears not when it’s forbidden — but when it’s no longer desired.

⚙️ T8 – Science & Ethics

Angle : Science sans conscience, création sans humanité.

Children are artificially produced and divided into castes (Alphas, Betas, Gammas, etc.). Ethics are sacrificed for stability and efficiency. It’s the triumph of the laboratory over the soul.

Phrase-type : Huxley’s world is one where ethics have been sacrificed on the altar of comfort.

💰 T10 – Inequality

Angle : Société divisée en castes, hiérarchie biologique et sociale programmée.

Inequality is not hidden — it’s justified as “scientific.” People are conditioned to love their place in the system. It’s a criticism of modern capitalism and consumerism disguised as stability.

Phrase-type : In Brave New World, inequality doesn’t need violence — it’s built into people’s DNA.

📺 T11 – Media & Truth

Angle : Propagande douce, distraction permanente, anesthésie du jugement.

The government doesn’t control people with lies, but with entertainment and drugs (soma). It’s the society of distraction — truth is irrelevant when everyone feels good. “Soma” is like social media today: an escape from reality that maintains control.

Phrase-type : As Huxley predicted, people would be enslaved not by pain, but by pleasure.

🎨 T14 – Art & Culture

Angle : Art supprimé, beauté remplacée par confort.

In Huxley’s world, art and literature are banned because they provoke emotion — and emotion threatens stability. It’s a warning against a world where beauty becomes dangerous.

Phrase-type : Where there is no art, there can be no humanity.

💬 Citations clés

  • “Community, Identity, Stability.” — (motto of the World State)
  • “Ending is better than mending.” — (consumerist slogan)
  • “You can’t consume much if you sit still and read books.”
  • “Words can be like X-rays if you use them properly — they’ll go through anything.”

Usage oral : Ces citations montrent la critique du conformisme et du consumérisme — à utiliser pour T1, T7, T10, T11.

💡 À retenir pour impressionner le jury

“Huxley’s world is not a nightmare of fear — it’s a dream of comfort that became a prison.”
