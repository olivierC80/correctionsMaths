📕 1984 (George Orwell, 1949)

🧠 T1 – Technology & Society

Angle : La technologie au service du contrôle total.

In 1984, technology is not a tool of progress — it’s a weapon of surveillance. Every home has a telescreen that spies on citizens. Orwell’s message is clear: when technology serves power instead of people, it destroys freedom.

Phrase-type : Orwell warned that technology can be used not to liberate minds, but to monitor them.

🗣️ T7 – Freedom of Speech / Thought

Angle : La liberté supprimée par le langage et la peur.

The regime controls thought through Newspeak, a language designed to make rebellion impossible. “Thoughtcrime” means that even private thoughts can be punished. Orwell shows that censorship begins inside the mind.

Phrase-type : As Orwell shows, the best way to silence people is not to forbid words — it’s to erase them.

⚙️ T8 – Science & Ethics

Angle : L’usage scientifique de la torture et du mensonge au service du pouvoir.

The Party uses psychological conditioning and technology to rewrite reality. Science becomes an instrument of domination — “2 + 2 = 5” if the Party says so. It’s a dystopian version of scientific power without moral restraint.

Phrase-type : Orwell’s world shows that when science serves ideology, truth itself becomes relative.

📺 T11 – Media & Truth

Angle : Propagande, falsification de l’histoire, manipulation de l’information.

“Who controls the past controls the future. Who controls the present controls the past.” The Party constantly rewrites history — a warning about the fragility of truth. The Ministry of Truth transforms lies into official facts — a perfect anticipation of “fake news.”

Phrase-type : In Orwell’s universe, truth isn’t hidden — it’s manufactured.

💰 T10 – Inequality

Angle : Société hiérarchisée : l’élite contrôle, le peuple obéit.

The “Inner Party” lives in luxury while the “proles” are kept ignorant. Inequality is maintained through propaganda, fear, and exhaustion — not just economics. It’s a timeless critique of totalitarian systems and the abuse of power.

Phrase-type : Orwell reminds us that inequality is easiest to preserve when people stop believing change is possible.

🎨 T14 – Art & Culture

Angle : La destruction de la culture et de la beauté comme acte de domination.

Poetry, literature, and art are forbidden because they inspire thought and emotion. “Big Brother” replaces imagination with ideology — the ultimate cultural sterilization.

Phrase-type : Without art, there is no memory — and without memory, there is no resistance.

💬 Citations clés

  • “Big Brother is watching you.”
  • “War is peace. Freedom is slavery. Ignorance is strength.”
  • “Who controls the past controls the future.”
  • “Freedom is the freedom to say that two plus two make four.”

Usage oral : Ces citations sont parfaites pour illustrer la surveillance, la manipulation du langage, et la défense de la vérité.
