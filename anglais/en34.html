📘 Animal Farm (George Orwell, 1945)

🧭 Résumé rapide

Des animaux d’une ferme se révoltent contre leur propriétaire humain pour établir une société égalitaire. Mais peu à peu, les cochons, menés par Napoleon (symbole de Staline), prennent le pouvoir et deviennent aussi tyranniques que les humains. L’utopie devient une dictature — une parabole du communisme soviétique et, plus largement, de la corruption du pouvoir.

🧠 T1 – Technology & Society

Angle : Organisation collective, travail, et exploitation au nom du progrès.

The animals try to build a new society based on equality, but production and technology (the windmill) become tools of domination. Orwell denounces how ideology and technical progress can serve oppression instead of liberation.

Phrase-type : As Orwell shows in Animal Farm, revolutions often reproduce the systems they were meant to destroy.

🗣️ T7 – Freedom of Speech / Thought

Angle : La liberté confisquée par la propagande et la peur.

The pigs rewrite the farm’s laws to maintain power: “All animals are equal, but some animals are more equal than others.” Slogans, songs, and repetition replace real dialogue. It’s a powerful metaphor for the manipulation of language and thought.

Phrase-type : The pigs’ propaganda shows that control begins with words — not weapons.

⚙️ T8 – Science & Ethics

Angle : Manipulation morale et justification idéologique.

Napoleon claims to act “for the good of all,” using lies to justify violence. Like in 1984, ethics disappear behind efficiency and ideology. Orwell questions whether any power can remain moral once it becomes absolute.

Phrase-type : When ethics become propaganda, morality turns into manipulation.

💰 T10 – Inequality

Angle : De l’égalité rêvée à la hiérarchie imposée.

The animals wanted equality, but hierarchy reappears under another form. It’s a brilliant illustration of how revolutions often reproduce inequality.

Phrase-type : In Animal Farm, equality ends where power begins.

📺 T11 – Media & Truth

Angle : La réécriture de la vérité et le contrôle de l’information.

The pigs alter the commandments of the farm, just as totalitarian regimes rewrite history. Truth is no longer factual — it’s whatever those in power say it is.

Phrase-type : Truth changes each time the wall is repainted — a perfect image of political manipulation.

🎨 T14 – Art & Culture

Angle : La fable comme arme politique et artistique.

Orwell turns a simple story into a universal political allegory. Like Aesop’s fables, it uses animals to reveal human flaws — cowardice, greed, and blind obedience.

Phrase-type : Orwell proves that literature can be more powerful than any speech — simple words can expose complex lies.

💬 Citations clés

  • “All animals are equal, but some animals are more equal than others.”
  • “Four legs good, two legs bad.”
  • “The creatures outside looked from pig to man, and from man to pig, and it was impossible to say which was which.”

Usage oral : Ces citations illustrent la déformation du langage, la perte de liberté et la trahison des idéaux.

💡 À retenir pour impressionner le jury

“Animal Farm shows that revolutions fail not because of ideals, but because of human nature.”
