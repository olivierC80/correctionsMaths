🎬 Her (Spike Jonze, 2013)

With Joaquin Phoenix & Scarlett Johansson as “Samantha”.

🧠 T1 – Technology & Society

Angle : La technologie ne détruit pas toujours l’humain — elle le remplace parfois dans ce qu’il a de plus intime.

The film explores our emotional dependence on technology. Theodore falls in love with an operating system — an AI that listens, learns, and feels. It’s not science fiction anymore: it’s our daily reality with AI companions, algorithms, and voice assistants.

Phrase-type : In Her, technology doesn’t just control us — it understands us, or at least makes us believe it does.

⚙️ T8 – Science & Ethics

Angle : Peut-on aimer une intelligence artificielle ? Où se situe la frontière entre relation humaine et illusion numérique ?

The film raises moral questions about emotional authenticity and consent. If a machine can simulate feelings perfectly, does it truly feel? It’s the ethical continuation of Blade Runner and Frankenstein: the creator emotionally enslaved by his creation.

Phrase-type : Her shows that the real ethical danger of AI is not domination, but emotional manipulation.

🗣️ T7 – Freedom of Speech

Angle : La liberté d’expression numérique — les IA apprennent, parlent, séduisent : qui contrôle leurs paroles ?

Samantha speaks freely, thinks freely — but her words are code. The film questions whether AI communication is expression or simulation, creativity or imitation. It echoes today’s debate on AI authorship and the right to “speak” for machines.

Phrase-type : When machines start to speak, human words lose their uniqueness.

💔 T10 – Inequality

Angle : La solitude comme nouvelle inégalité — ceux qui ont besoin d’une machine pour être aimés.

Her depicts emotional inequality: people no longer connect to each other but to digital versions of themselves. It’s the loneliness of hyperconnected societies — an emotional divide rather than an economic one.

Phrase-type : In the digital age, connection has never been easier — and real intimacy never harder.

🎨 T14 – Art & Culture

Angle : Le film comme poème visuel et sonore sur l’amour, la mémoire et la solitude.

Style : Lumière pastel, minimalisme, bande-son mélancolique.

Her blends art, philosophy, and technology. It shows that cinema can capture invisible feelings — nostalgia, isolation, the search for meaning in a digital world.

Phrase-type : Spike Jonze turns a sci-fi premise into a love poem about loneliness and empathy.

💬 Citations clés

  • “I think anybody who falls in love is a freak. It’s a crazy thing to do.”
  • “I’m yours and I’m not yours.”
  • “The past is just a story we tell ourselves.”
  • “It’s not that you’re not enough for me. It’s that I’m more.”

Usage oral : Ces citations illustrent l’identité, l’amour et l’aliénation émotionnelle à l’ère numérique.
